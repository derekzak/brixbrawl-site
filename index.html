Brix Brawl Support

A local two-player fighting game for iPad · Privacy Policy

Need help or found a bug? Email zak.support12@gmail.com and include your iPad model and iPadOS version. We read every message.

How do I play?

Hold the iPad in landscape between two players. Player 1 uses the controls on the left, Player 2 on the right: arrows to move, plus punch, kick and block. Empty your opponent's health bar to win.

Is there a single-player mode?

Not yet. Brix Brawl is built for two people sharing one iPad.

What do POWER, SPEED and GUARD do?

POWER raises damage and knockback, SPEED raises walking speed and makes attacks recover faster, and GUARD reduces damage taken and block stun. Every fighter's stats add up to the same total, so each is a different trade-off.

The game says "Rotate to landscape"

Brix Brawl is played side by side, so turn your iPad sideways and the game appears.

There's no sound

Check the volume, and make sure your iPad isn't in Silent mode. The game respects the silent switch and Control Center setting.

Does the game collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or internet connection. See the Privacy Policy.
