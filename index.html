Brix Brawl Support

A fighting game for iPhone and iPad: solo vs CPU or two players on one device · Privacy Policy

Need help or found a bug? Email zak.support12@gmail.com and include your device model (iPhone or iPad) and iOS version. We read every message.

How do I play?

Hold the device in landscape. For two players, place it between you. Player 1 uses the controls on the left, Player 2 on the right: arrows to move, plus punch, kick and block. Empty your opponent's health bar to win.

Is there a single-player mode?

Yes, from version 1.1. Tap 1 PLAYER on the title screen to fight a CPU opponent. On Choose Your Fighter, tap the CPU's difficulty button to switch between Easy, Normal and Hard.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, from version 1.2. Brix Brawl runs on iPhone and iPad in landscape, with a layout tuned for each screen. It is one purchase for both: buy it once with your Apple ID and download it on every iPhone and iPad you use (Family Sharing applies if you have it on). 2 PLAYERS on an iPhone works too, but the screen is small, so an iPad is roomier for two.

What do POWER, SPEED and GUARD do?

POWER raises damage and knockback, SPEED raises walking speed and makes attacks recover faster, and GUARD reduces damage taken and block stun. Every fighter's stats add up to the same total, so each is a different trade-off.

The game says "Rotate to landscape"

Brix Brawl is played side by side, so turn your iPhone or iPad sideways and the game appears.

There's no sound

Check the volume, and make sure your device isn't in Silent mode. The game respects the silent switch and Control Center setting.

Does the game collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or internet connection. See the Privacy Policy.
