Brix Brawl Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026 · Support

Brix Brawl is a local two-player fighting game for iPad, made by Derek Zak ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles. The short version: Brix Brawl does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Brix Brawl has no accounts, sign-in, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or third-party SDKs, and it never connects to the internet. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or usage data.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a few game settings, such as sound, music and haptics preferences, on your iPad so they're remembered between sessions. These settings never leave your device, are not sent to us or anyone else, and are deleted when you delete the app.

Tracking

Brix Brawl does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier.

Children's privacy

Brix Brawl is suitable for families. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Purchases

If you buy Brix Brawl, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles that information.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and change the effective date above before that version is released.
